LeadFlow / Call Script
7-Step Call Script
Commercial Insurance Call Script — Outsurance commercial
01
Opening
"Good [morning/afternoon], may I speak with the owner or the person who handles your business insurance? My name is [Name] from Outsurance."
02
The Hook
"I'm reaching out to [Company] specifically because we work with [Industry] businesses in [City] and we've been helping them save 15–35% on premiums while improving their cover."
03
Pain Question
"When last did someone sit down and properly review your business insurance? Most owners we speak to haven't had a review in 2+ years — and they're overpaying."
04
Handle "We're happy"
"That's great — I'm not asking you to change anything today. Just a FREE 15-minute comparison. Worst case: you confirm you've got a great deal. Best case: we save you thousands."
05
The Close
"I can have a no-obligation quote within 24 hours. All I need is your annual premium figure. Would [day] morning or afternoon work for a quick 15-minute call?"
06
Objection: "Send info first"
"Absolutely — what's the best email? To make it relevant, what's your approximate annual insurance spend? Even a rough figure helps me personalise it for [Company]."
07
The Wrap
"Perfect. I'll get that to you by [time]. Just to confirm — [Company], [Industry], in [City]. Anything specific about your cover — vehicles, premises, liability, professional indemnity?"
