📞 7-Step Call Script
Proven Outsurance commercial insurance opening
01
The Opening
"Good [morning/afternoon], may I speak with the owner or the person who handles your business insurance? My name is [Your Name] from Outsurance."
02
The Hook
"I'm reaching out to [Company] specifically because we work with a lot of [Industry] businesses in [City] and we've been helping them save between 15% and 35% on their commercial premiums while actually improving their cover."
03
The Pain Question
"Can I ask — when last did someone actually sit down and review your business insurance to make sure you're not over-paying or under-covered? Most business owners we speak to haven't had a proper review in over 2 years."
04
Handle "We're happy with current insurer"
"That's great to hear — I'm not asking you to change anything today. I'd just like to do a FREE 15-minute comparison so you can see if you're getting the best value. The worst case is you confirm you've got a great deal. The best case is we save you thousands."
05
The Close
"I can have a no-obligation quote back to you within 24 hours. All I need is your current policy schedule — even just the premium amount. Would [day] morning or [day] afternoon work for a quick 15-minute call?"
06
Objection: "Send me info first"
"Absolutely — what's the best email? And just so I can make the info relevant to [Company], what's your approximate annual insurance spend? Even a rough figure helps me personalise it for you."
07
The Wrap
"Perfect. I'll get that across to you by [time] today. Just to confirm — [Company], [Industry], based in [City]. Is there anything specific about your cover you'd want me to focus on — vehicles, premises, liability?"
